# Lijst Support

## Need Help?

Thank you for using **Lijst**!

Lijst is a simple and private to-do list app designed to help you organize your tasks and keep track of your progress.

If you have any questions, encounter a problem, or have feedback about the app, please contact us.

## Contact

**Email:** support@lijst.com

When contacting us about an issue, please include:

- A brief description of the problem
- The steps that caused the issue
- Your iPhone/iPad model
- Your iOS/iPadOS version
- The version of Lijst you are using

Please do not include any sensitive or personal information in your support request.

## Privacy

Lijst does not collect or transmit your personal information or task data. Your tasks are stored locally on your device.

For more information, please see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## Feedback

We'd love to hear your feedback and suggestions for improving Lijst. Feel free to contact us using the email address above.
