
# Privacy Policy for Lijst

**Effective Date:** October 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Lijst ("the App") is a simple to-do list application designed to help users organize tasks and track their completion. Your privacy is important to us, and the App is designed to operate without collecting personal information.

## 2. Information Collection

Lijst does not collect, request, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details.

## 3. Data Storage

All tasks and related information created in Lijst are stored locally on the user's device. The App does not send this information to external servers or store it in a remote database.

## 4. Data Sharing

Lijst does not sell, share, disclose, or transmit user data to third parties. No task information or personal information is sent to the developer or any external service by the App.

## 5. Analytics and Tracking

Lijst does not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, or tracking technologies to monitor user activity.

## 6. Data Security and Deletion

Since task information is stored locally on your device, its security depends partly on your device's security settings. You can manage or delete your tasks within the App. Deleting the App may also remove locally stored data, depending on how iOS manages that data and whether it is included in a device backup.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Lijst does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the App does not collect personal information.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on the webpage where this policy is hosted, along with a revised effective date.

## 9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer through the support contact provided on the Lijst App Store page.
